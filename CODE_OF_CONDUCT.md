# Code of Conduct

DeltaForge is a learning project. Participation should make it easier for people to ask
questions, test ideas, and improve their work without being belittled.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Welcome people across experience levels, backgrounds, identities, and abilities.
- Keep critique about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior is inappropriate. Enforcement decisions should consider
context, impact, and whether harmful behavior continues after a clear request to stop.

Report conduct concerns confidentially through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/eloidieme/DeltaForge/security/advisories/new),
and state at the start that the report concerns conduct rather than a vulnerability.
Include links or screenshots when useful. Reports will be handled as privately as the
investigation allows, and retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies in all project spaces and whenever someone publicly represents the
project.
